About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery began in 2018 in a modest garage with a small stone mill, a vintage deck oven, and an obsession with long fermentation. Over the past eight years, we have grown into a lively neighborhood gathering space where neighbors catch up over morning coffee and commuters pick up crusty loaves on their way home.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We source grains exclusively from regional farmers throughout the Pacific Northwest who practice regenerative soil stewardship. Our flours are stone-ground to retain the germ and bran, yielding breads that are flavorful and easier to digest.
