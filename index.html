Welcome to North Star Bakery

We are a neighborhood bakery crafting slow-fermented artisan sourdough, morning pastries, and custom celebration cakes every single morning.

What We're Known For

Our kitchen relies on stone-ground regional grains, wild sourdough starters, and pure butter. We believe in unhurried fermentation methods that produce deep crusts, chewy crumbs, and clean flavors without artificial preservatives.
